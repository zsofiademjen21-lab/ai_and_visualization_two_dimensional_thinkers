What we found: 3 key takeaways

Takeaway 1

The model often fails to recognise disturbed galaxies

Examples: disturbed galaxies the model got wrong

Takeaway 2

Small disturbed galaxies get mistaken for ellipticals

Examples: small disturbed galaxies the model called Elliptical

Likely reason: in a small image the disturbed structure isn't visible, so the galaxy looks like a smooth blob, which is what an elliptical looks like.

Takeaway 3

The model is often very sure even when it is wrong

Examples: the model's most confident mistakes (true label → model's answer)

Some of these may be wrong labels rather than model mistakes. For example, some "spirals" are clearly two galaxies merging. We still need to check them by hand.

Data: Galaxy10 DECals validation images (not used for training). Numbers are calculated live from the model's predictions. For details, open explorer.html.
