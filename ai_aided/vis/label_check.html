What does each class look like?
Spiral: a disk with spiral arms winding out from the centre, sometimes with a bar across the middle.
Elliptical: a smooth round or oval glow with no arms or structure.
Edge-on Disk: a flat disk seen from the side, a thin line or narrow streak, sometimes with a central bulge.
Irregular (disturbed): a single galaxy with a lopsided, distorted or messy shape.
Merger: two or more galaxies touching or pulling at each other, often with tails or bridges of light.
